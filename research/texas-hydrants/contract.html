Goal and decision

Establish a verified inventory of fire-hydrant coordinate data sources across all 254 Texas counties. Determine where those sources support automated straight-line distance calculations from a property to its nearest hydrant, without interactive portal use. Identify sources, demonstrate retrieval, assess geographic coverage and completeness, document gaps and acquisition options, and preserve reproducible evidence for another agent.

Research completion and hydrant inventory coverage are separate fields and separate gates. A county can have completed research and still have incomplete data. A blocked search cannot establish either source absence or hydrant absence. This run's current status is blocked and incomplete.

Discovery method

The atomic unit is one named publisher or one bounded search within a county and source family. Workers save replayable inputs under incoming/; a single consolidator changes data/. Work through one unit, retain the request/response and source URLs, classify each lead, check the changed records, then choose the next unit.

The 1,524 generated family slots are a search plan, not executed research. Before closing a county, identify its incorporated municipalities, relevant water suppliers and districts, fire authorities, and regional coverage from source-backed directories. Include providers whose service territory crosses county lines. Each identified entity needs an investigated source, a justified exclusion, or an unresolved lead with an explicit blocker and next action. Entity enumeration itself must have saved evidence and a rationale for its completeness. A county-name keyword search alone is insufficient.

A discovery task becomes complete only after its enumerated entities and searches are reviewed. A finished investigation may conclude that a particular inventory requires a bulk request or authentication; that is a documented data gap. An environmental failure to run the investigation leaves the task unfinished. Failed requests retain their error and may not be recorded as successful searches with no results.

Source registry and retrieval evidence

Each source records ID, publisher attribution, dataset/layer ID, API/download URL, access method, format, native coordinate system or explicit uncertainty, geographic scope, update information, reuse terms or uncertainty, verification timestamp, disposition, duplicate lineage, evidence references, inventory completeness and omissions. Possible dispositions are usable, restricted, unavailable, duplicate, irrelevant and unresolved. An unknown field is explicit; it is never silently guessed. Publisher attribution based on third-party code stays qualified until official verification.

Usable requires a saved reproducible request and an actual nonempty response containing valid hydrant point coordinates, publisher/layer identity confirmation, coordinate-system and axis-order validation, an expected-location check, and a described bulk retrieval method. Keep raw response bytes and SHA256. Current automated coordinate checks consume WGS84 GeoJSON; for other formats retain raw data, conversion command, native CRS and normalized GeoJSON. A successful HTTP response containing HTML, an ArcGIS error, null geometry, a screenshot, metadata only, fabricated fixtures or a generic map is not usable evidence. Never save tokens or credentials in requests.

For ArcGIS, inspect layer metadata, geometry type, fields, capabilities, spatial reference, update dates, object ID field and maxRecordCount. Request a small geometry sample with outSR=4326. Compare samples with independently evidenced expected geography. For bulk retrieval prefer a complete object-ID list and bounded ID batches when supported; otherwise use stable ordered pagination and verify counts, uniqueness, exceededTransferLimit and every page. Preserve timestamps and handle changing inventories. Sampling success and complete export are distinct achievements.

For a downloadable file retain the original and metadata, identify point geometry and CRS, convert reproducibly, validate locations and count dropped/null records. For all sources check licensing and attribution. Unknown reuse terms remain a limitation. Do not request access, pay, send email or file records requests without user authorization; draft the specific acquisition action in the backlog.

Store observed point locations separately from publisher-claimed inventory territory. A point bounding box or administrative boundary establishes neither inventory completeness nor service eligibility. Record public/private systems, inactive/proposed assets, temporal omissions, positional uncertainty and neighboring jurisdictions explicitly. Report a percentage only with its unit, denominator, method and evidence. The current map shows county research status only; there is no defensible hydrant-coverage percentage or inventory territory map yet.

Distance calculations use an explicit geocoded property point and validated hydrant coordinates in a known CRS. Compute geodesic distance in metres (or document a local projected method and conversion), not road distance. Query across county and utility boundaries. Unless completeness is established in the relevant search area, label results nearest observed hydrant; an incomplete inventory can miss a closer hydrant. Distances do not certify flow, accessibility, serviceability or insurance qualification. No production distance calculator is accepted in this run without real validated source data.

Acceptance and independent review

  1. Exactly the official 254-county name/FIPS set is present, with research status, linked sources, coverage status and actionable gaps.
  2. All six discovery families and their entity enumerations are completed for every county. Searches and all lead dispositions have evidence.
  3. Every source has required registry fields and documented lineage or uncertainty.
  4. Every usable source has actual coordinate retrieval evidence, location validation and a reproducible bulk method.
  5. Every completeness claim has independent evidence of territory and inventory completeness; observed points remain distinct.
  6. Coverage uncertainty and omissions are explicit. No-source and incomplete-search states cannot be conflated.
  7. Machine-readable data, county table, evidence, appropriate map, acquisition backlog and exact restart steps agree.
  8. Integrity checks and independent review pass. The reviewer audits every complete-coverage claim, all sources supporting those claims, and a reproducible representative sample of usable and no-source findings. Stratify samples by region, urban/rural scope, publisher and retrieval format; record sampling method/seed, selected IDs, assertions checked and corrections. Sparse groups should be fully reviewed. With zero eligible findings, record sample unavailable and do not assert statewide acceptance.

validate.py tests integrity of an unfinished corpus. validate.py --acceptance is the separate, stricter statewide completion gate. A passing structural check is not acceptance. Human or independent-agent judgment still checks publisher claims, entity enumeration, source identity, sample representativeness and the truth of evidence; schema validation alone cannot establish those facts.
