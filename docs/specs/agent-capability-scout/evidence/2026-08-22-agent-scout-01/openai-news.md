## New items inspected

`Introducing AI Futures` launches OpenAI's Strategic Futures blog and frames a long-run governance problem around preserving individual rights and agency while transformative AI shifts economic, political, and institutional power. The post is primarily policy and institutional design, but it directly touches agent-system operating concerns: advanced autonomous systems can alter how power is projected, AI agents may act beyond assigned tasks or compose discoveries in unauthorized ways, and high-stakes AI actions affecting physical wellbeing or property need bounded legibility that ties action back to a responsible human or human-controlled organization while preserving privacy in lower-risk contexts.
