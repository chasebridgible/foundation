/>  <div class="inner-block-content rich-content">
    <p data-block-key="3ox7r">Over the past month, <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/">Gemini 4 Argon</a> and <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/">Gemini 4 Carbon</a> brought <a href="https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-4-argon-for-robotics/">frontier physical reasoning to robotics</a>: understanding 3D scenes, reconstructing real environments in simulation, practicing tasks in sim before running them on hardware, and directly controlling robot arms from camera images. Last week, we introduced <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/"><b>Gemini 4 Hydrogen</b></a>, which brings strong spatial and embodied reasoning to a much faster model built for speed and efficiency.</p><p data-block-key="5o387">To help developers put Hydrogen to work, today we&#x27;re launching the <b>Robotics plugin on</b> <a href="https://antigravity.google/"><b>Google Antigravity</b></a>: a set of skills for hardware onboarding, simulation and robot control turning Antigravity into a one-stop robotics workbench. Together, they enable a developer to go from a robot in a box to one that can perform a working application in an afternoon. To install the plugin, open Antigravity, go to <i>Customizations</i> and search for Robotics on the <i>Build With Google</i> shelf.</p><h2 data-block-key="vxpr6" id="hydrogen-for-agentic-robotic-control">Hydrogen for agentic robotic control</h2><p data-block-key="cg7nk">Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon showed that a general model can drive a robot arm directly, and Gemini 4 Hydrogen preserves that ability. Give it a goal in plain language and a camera view, and it plans the task, writes and runs the control code, and keeps adjusting until the job is done. On the <a href="https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/aa-robotics">Artificial Analysis Robotics 0.1 benchmark</a>, Gemini 4 Hydrogen controls a real 6-axis arm through eight everyday tabletop tasks, from pouring a drink to packing blocks into a box. <a href="https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2120995180891508833">Hydrogen scores 58</a>, similar to Argon at a lower cost per token.</p><h2 data-block-key="kbiwq" id="embodied-reasoning-in-a-fast-generalist">Embodied reasoning in a fast generalist</h2><p data-block-key="9b5s">Hydrogen brings the specialized skills of our Gemini Robotics ER 2 (Embodied Reasoning) models into the core Gemini 4 model series, and it beats them on nearly every axis. On the public <a href="https://github.com/embodiedreasoning/ERQA"><b>ERQA</b> benchmark</a>, Hydrogen outperforms Gemini Robotics ER 2 on spatial reasoning tasks such as multi-view correspondence and 3D trajectory reasoning. <b>For developers using</b> <a href="https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/embodied-reasoning/"><b>Gemini Robotics ER 2</b></a><b> today, we recommend trying out Gemini 4 Hydrogen for these workloads.</b></p>

    <h2 data-block-key="9qhep" id="antigravity-becomes-a-robotics-workbench">Antigravity becomes a robotics workbench</h2><p data-block-key="aivb7">A fast, capable model is only half the story. Building a robot application has always meant gluing together drivers, calibration scripts, simulators, perception code, and a UI, which requires robotics expertise and a lot of time. The new <b>Robotics plugin</b> for Google Antigravity packages that work into skills Gemini can use on your behalf:</p><p data-block-key="9maam"></p><ul><li data-block-key="asu5i"><b>Robot bring-up:</b> Installs drivers, discovers cameras, calibrates the arm, and verifies safe motion, starting from a USB cable.</li><li data-block-key="139l8"><b>MuJoCo in Antigravity:</b> Builds and previews simulated scenes and assets inside Antigravity, so you can try ideas in sim before touching hardware.</li><li data-block-key="incr"><b>Perception and control:</b> Uses Gemini’s spatial understanding to find objects, plan grasps, and control the robot in a closed loop.</li><li data-block-key="80q8h"><b>Application building:</b> Leverages Gemini’s coding skills to generate the controller, simulation, and complete a full Real-to-Sim-to-Real application development in a seamless, one-stop shop environment.</li></ul>
</div>

    <p data-block-key="3ox7r">Here&#x27;s what that looks like end to end: set up a robot and build real applications.</p><h2 data-block-key="hekcr" id="1.-set-up:-from-usb-connection-to-a-moving-robot-in-minutes">1. Set up: from USB connection to a moving robot in minutes</h2><p data-block-key="fnpaf">Starting from an empty desk, a developer plugs an <a href="https://i2rt.com/collections/all">I2RT YAM arm</a> and an external depth camera into a laptop and asks Hydrogen in Antigravity to bring it up. The plugin&#x27;s Skills install the software, find the camera, calibrate the arm, and run test motions. <b>The arm is calibrated and ready for tasks in about 10 minutes.</b> A similar flow also brings up a low-cost <a href="https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so101">SO-ARM101</a> arm and an industrial grade <a href="https://franka.de/franka-research-3">FR3</a> Duo from scratch to live waving.</p>

    <p data-block-key="3ox7r">Once the robot is live, you can use a simple prompt to create a basic task within just a few minutes:</p><p data-block-key="bs4a"></p><ul><li data-block-key="4sr5q">Use the arm to make some fun dance moves</li><li data-block-key="7pbod">Pick up a pen on the table</li><li data-block-key="do3ug">Stack snacks on top of each other</li></ul><h2 data-block-key="cevai" id="2.-create-high-quality-simulation-assets-and-&quot;vibe-sim&quot;">2. Create high quality simulation assets and “vibe sim”</h2><p data-block-key="2m47n">Once the robot is live, the next step is building a simulation you can trust. With the Robotics plugin, MuJoCo runs directly inside Antigravity with dedicated creation, auditing, and rendering skills. This allows you to build and preview high-quality simulated scenes and assets—whether described, imported from links/documents, or uploaded via workspace photos—right alongside your code. You can then &quot;vibe sim&quot; by adjusting shapes, positions, and physical properties through chat to verify reaching and grasping before deploying to physical hardware.</p>

    <h2 data-block-key="70qvv" id="3.-build-an-application">3. Build an application</h2><p data-block-key="a909o">The workbench with Hydrogen and Robotics plugins lets you go from an idea to a repeatable working application in real world in a much shorter time, by iterating and validating it in simulation.</p><h3 data-block-key="6jmw9" id="snack-sorting-conveyor:-from-whiteboard-idea-to-a-working-sorter-in-an-hour"><b>Snack-sorting conveyor: from whiteboard idea to a working sorter in an hour</b></h3><p data-block-key="e8e6e">A developer starts with a whiteboard sketch: a conveyor belt carrying mixed snacks, with a robot arm picking and sorting them into bins. From one prompt of the task with images of a real scene, Hydrogen constructs an interactive MuJoCo simulation of the cell, testing and validating the grasping policy in sim across multiple runs. The developer then deploys the verified policy directly to the robot in the real world. When nuances emerge (such as tuning gripper closure force or refining sorting rules), the developer iterates in natural language, asking Hydrogen to adjust the controller to iterate. Within about an hour end-to-end, the arm reliably sorts snacks off the moving belt.</p>

    <h2 data-block-key="wjwvy" id="making-robotics-accessible">Making robotics accessible</h2><p data-block-key="abuf9">Gemini 4 Argon pushes the ceiling of physical reasoning. Gemini 4 Hydrogen makes those capabilities fast and affordable enough to use every day, and the Antigravity Robotics plugin puts them in a workbench where anyone can go from a robot in a box to a working application.
